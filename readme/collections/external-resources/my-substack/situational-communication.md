# Situational Communication:

### When to Say "This is Wrong"

[Anan](https://substack.com/@anan12)Dec 28, 2024

When you want to change something, would you say this is wrong or you would say there is a better way?(it depends?)

Is this always correct?

No, this isn't always correct. While using positive language and focusing on improvement is generally a good approach, there are situations where directly addressing a problem as "wrong" is necessary. For example, in situations with ethical violations or safety concerns, using direct language is crucial for clarity and urgency. (in science and engineering realm we should not mind this positive sentiments, or in a proper team you can be blunt)

The context and severity of the situation should guide your choice of language.

Think: Why do we say "Smoking causes cancer" instead of "Not smoking leads to a life with less risk of lung cancer?"
