# Spilt milk under the bridge

[Anan](https://substack.com/@anan12)Oct 28, 2026

> "Spilt milk under the bridge" is a malaphor—a mix of two common English idioms: "don't cry over spilt milk" and "water under the bridge".
>
> Meaning of the Combined Idioms
>
> • Don't cry over spilt milk: Do not waste time worrying or feeling sad about a mistake or accident that has already happened and cannot be fixed.
>
> • Water under the bridge: Events from the past that are finished, forgiven, or no longer important.
>
> • The Malaphor: Putting them together means acknowledging a past event or error that cannot be undone, while deciding not to stress or dwell on it anymore.
