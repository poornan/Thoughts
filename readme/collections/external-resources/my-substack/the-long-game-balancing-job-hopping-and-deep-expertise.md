# The Long Game: Balancing Job Hopping and Deep Expertise

[Anan](https://substack.com/@anan12)Feb 15, 2025

The Allure of Quick Gains: Short-Term vs. Long-Term Growth

I stayed in my first corporate job for 2.5 years. I wasn't earning much then, but I was learning. My peers started moving to new jobs in six months with about a 0.5 times increment. Meanwhile, I understood development and testing methodologies, processes, organizational bureaucracy, and power plays. I broadened my knowledge from networking and testing to programming and cybersecurity. My next job offered a 2.5 times increase in base salary and a 3.5 times increase in gross salary. Definitely, there is financial value in jumping jobs often, and it has a compounding effect. But knowledge doesn't compound unless you spend a reasonable time with the problems, understanding and seeing them through to the end.
